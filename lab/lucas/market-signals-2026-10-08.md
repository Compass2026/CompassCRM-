## Objections
- **Cost shock and quotes that are hard to compare.** NRCA says price is only one criterion; compare the written scope.
- **Insurance confusion.** ACV vs RCV, wear-and-tear denials, partial repairs.
- **Fear of post-storm scams.** Upfront cash and abandoned jobs (MO AG, May 2026; BBB, April 2026).
- **Timing.** Can it wait until spring? Can a roof go on in the cold?
- **Hassle.** Days of noise and debris, and protecting the yard.

## Timely angles, eastern Missouri, Oct–Nov 2026
- **Fraud case.** On May 28 2026 the MO Attorney General charged a contractor who took upfront payments from St. Louis tornado victims for work never finished. Treat this as an allegation and never name the company.
- **RSMo 407.725 (statute text verified).** A contractor "shall not advertise or promise to pay or rebate all or any portion of any insurance deductible". It "shall not represent or negotiate … on behalf of an owner … on any insurance claim". The insured may cancel within five business days after written notice that the claim is not a covered loss.
- **Claim deadlines.** These depend on the policy **[uncertain]**. Missouri DCI's do's and don'ts: document the damage, make temporary repairs only, keep receipts.
- **Fall checkup from the ground (NRCA).** Watch for granules collecting in the gutters, and for gutters and leaves straining the fascia.
- **Holiday lights.** Use clips rather than nails or staples, and follow basic ladder safety.

## Myths worth correcting
1. "The roofer will negotiate with my insurer for me." Missouri law bars that.
2. "They'll cover my deductible." Missouri law prohibits that.
3. "No visible damage from the ground means no damage."
4. "Roofs can't be replaced in cold weather."
5. "Just shingle over the old roof." Code generally caps a roof at two layers, and most manufacturers advise against overlaying laminated shingles.
6. "The lowest bid is the best value."
